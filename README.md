## Cortiva

A day planner with an assistant that re-plans around delays, meetings and low energy, and asks before it changes anything. Timeline, map and the assistant all work around the same real day. I do the product, the app, the backend and every decision in between, and I am still going. Open to a team, licensing or acquisition that wants to take it further.

## Island Desktop

<a href="https://github.com/endrymagolas-wq/gnome-dynamic-island"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/endrymagolas-wq/gnome-dynamic-island/main/docs/images/site/01-full-desktop.jpg" alt="Island Desktop for Windows: a Dynamic Island, floating dock, media and AirPlay over a living lagoon" width="100%"></a>

Nobody asked for this. I wanted my computer to do a few things my way: a Dynamic Island with media controls, a floating dock, AirPlay from the phone and a living island as the wallpaper. Started on Linux as a GNOME Shell extension, now also a native Windows app. Free and open source, GPL-3.0.

[Repository](https://github.com/endrymagolas-wq/gnome-dynamic-island) · [Downloads](https://github.com/endrymagolas-wq/gnome-dynamic-island/releases/tag/windows-v0.4.0-beta.2) · [41-second video](https://github.com/endrymagolas-wq/gnome-dynamic-island/releases/download/windows-v0.4.0-beta.2/island-desktop.mp4)
